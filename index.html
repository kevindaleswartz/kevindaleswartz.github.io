Bookkeeper

Bookkeeper is a personal Windows program written for its owner's own use. It keeps track of bills, bank accounts, receipts, documents, forms, notes, reminders and calendars on the owner's own computer.

Bookkeeper can connect to the user's own Google Calendar so that events can be viewed, added and changed from inside the program.

Bookkeeper is not sold or offered to the public.
